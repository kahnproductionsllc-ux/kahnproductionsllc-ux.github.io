Privacy Policy

Kahn Productions LLC · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Kahn Productions LLC ("we", "us") runs a small private tool that helps a few business clients receive a daily list of sales leads in their own email. This policy explains what that tool does with Google account data.

What we access

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, we ask Google only for permission to create email drafts in your account (the Gmail "compose" permission). We use it for one thing: saving your daily lead report as a draft in your Gmail Drafts folder so you can review it.

What we do not do

  • We never send email from your account. Every report is saved as a draft, and you decide whether to send anything.
  • We do not read, search, delete or change your existing email.
  • We do not see or store your Google password.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we store

We store only the sign-in token Google gives us and your email address, on a private computer controlled by Kahn Productions LLC, so the daily draft can be created. We do not keep copies of your mailbox.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to disconnect

You can remove our access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, or ask us to and we will delete your stored token.
